Working from the cell up.

I'm a UNSW Computer Science / Bioinformatics graduate (first‑class honours, 86) building a career around computational methods for cancer biology. My recent work has spanned spatial transcriptomics in mouse brain, multi‑modal machine learning for hepatocellular carcinoma recurrence, and statistical modelling of the human microbiome.

In September I begin a PhD with Martin Smith at the Ramaciotti Centre, working on long-read isoform-level quantification in cancer and immuno-oncology. Until then, I'm available for a focused 3–4 month contract — ideally somewhere whose pipelines and methods build the skills I'll need on day one of the PhD.

I care about reproducible, well-documented analysis and about choosing the simplest tool that honestly answers the biological question. I also write — about methods I'm replicating, tools I'm learning, and the small surprises that show up in the data.
